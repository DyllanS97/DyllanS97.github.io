Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Who uses Hugo

Hugo is a private assistant used only by its owner. No other people can sign in or use it.

What data Hugo accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Hugo uses the Gmail API with the gmail.modify scope to read the owner's own mail and labels, and to apply labels, archive, move to trash or mark as spam. Hugo cannot send mail from, forward from, or permanently delete anything in the owner's Gmail.

How the data is used

Mail is used only to give the owner summaries and suggestions about their own inbox. Actions on mail are proposed to the owner and carried out only after approval.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed and stored on hardware the owner controls. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties. Hugo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Hugo's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on Hugo's Google consent screen.
